Questions Before You Join?

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining is free. You do not need to buy anything to promote RentReady.

What am I selling?

Two products: the RentReady Essential Guide ($39.99) and the RentReady Complete Bundle ($79.99). Both help people learn about credit, rental checks, and getting ready to apply for a home.

Do I need to know about credit or real estate?

No. The product does the teaching. You just share your link with people it can help.

Do I need 2,500 followers?

We recommend 2,500+ combined followers, but it is not required.

How do I get paid?

Sales made through your link are tracked through ClickBank. You are paid for qualifying sales under ClickBank's payment terms.

Am I guaranteed to make money?

No. You earn when people buy through your link. Your results depend on how you promote your link and who you reach.
